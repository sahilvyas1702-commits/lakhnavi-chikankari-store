WHY LAKHNAVI

An embroidery-led wardrobe

Chikankari is strongly associated with Lucknow and is admired for thread-led surface work that can feel delicate, graphic or richly layered depending on the cloth and colour.

Lakhnavi Chikankari is a curated storefront. We select pieces for their overall embroidery, colour and wearability, then help customers confirm the practical details—measurements, fabric, availability and delivery—before an order is finalised.
