PUBLISH ON A FREE NETLIFY SUBDOMAIN
===================================

1. Sign in to Netlify and choose Add new project → Deploy manually.
2. Extract the ZIP file on your computer.
3. Drag the extracted website folder into Netlify's deployment area.
4. If Netlify creates the project as private, publish it from the project page.
5. Open Domain management → Production domains → Options → Edit site name.
6. Choose an available name. The address will look like:
   https://YOUR-SITE-NAME.netlify.app
7. Update the Supabase Authentication URLs described in README.txt.
8. Add the server-only variables from README.txt before enabling online payment.

IMPORTANT
---------
- The netlify.app address is the free domain-like website address.
- A custom .com or .in is a paid registration and normally renews every year.
- Netlify's free-plan limits, availability and terms apply.
- Keep this entire folder or ZIP. It is your editable source copy for future
  changes, even if you do not publish it now.
