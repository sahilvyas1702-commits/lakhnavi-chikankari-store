GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions

How do I confirm my size?+

Share your height, weight or a similar fitted garment on WhatsApp. We confirm measurements and current stock for the specific piece before you pay.

Can I order on WhatsApp instead of paying online?+

Yes. Add pieces to your bag and choose “Order this bag on WhatsApp”. We will confirm size, colour and availability, then share payment or COD options.

What are the delivery times?+

Delivery timing depends on the destination and the selected piece. Availability is confirmed before dispatch and you will receive the expected timeline on WhatsApp.

How do I look after hand-embroidered kurtis?+

Turn the garment inside out and wash gently by hand or dry clean, following the care label supplied. Never wring embroidered areas. See the care guide for details.

Is online payment secure?+

Payments run through Razorpay's protected checkout with card, UPI and net banking. Orders are verified on the server before being confirmed.
