DISCOVER THE CRAFT

The beauty of Chikankari

A heritage of delicate embroidery

Chikankari is closely associated with Lucknow and admired for its fine, thread-led surface work. The beauty often lies in the quiet detail—floral patterns, light textures and careful repetition.

Choosing your everyday style

Soft colours bring an understated mood, while deeper tones make the embroidery feel more graphic. Choose by colour first, then ask us to confirm current sizes and available variations.

Caring for handwork

Embroidered clothing benefits from gentle handling. Follow the care information shared with the specific piece, and contact us whenever you are unsure about washing or storage.
